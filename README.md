# DeFi Connect Credit AI-powered DEX and Rwa Lending.

This is a platform where AI + DeFI + RWA combined. AI powered The AMM and The RWA Lending platform.
With the AI agent you could Trade, Borrow and Lending seamlessly.

**Features:**
1. DEX
2. Rwa Lending platform
3. AI Agent.


 ### 1. how does The DEX  works ?

This is just like the Uniswap V2 AMM whereby Liquidity Providers provide Liquidity and  Users can swap Their crypto assets.

 ### 2. how does the RWA lending Works?

This is a Lending platform that connect Borrowers to the Lenders. Borrower Request Loan  by providing collateral which could be a Supported RWA assets by our platform or Cryto assets and The Lenders lend out their assets which could also be a Crypto assets or RWA  to them with 100% secured of their Assets.

**How do we protect the Lenders' funds?**

There is a Liquidation process which occurs in two ways:
1. **Time-based period Expiration**
2. **Undercollateralization**

 **Time-based:** In our platform we give 1 month period for the Borrower to repay the loan and if the time elaspes the Position is Open to Liquidation.
 **Undercollateralization:** In our platform if the position of a particular Borrower is Undercollarized, it will open to Liquidation, That means if the Position is below the LiquidationThreshold.

**how does the Liqudaton work in our Platform?**
Once a Position is liquidated in either of the ways be it Time-based or Undercollateralization which made the Position open to Liquidation for everyone, the Borrower Collateral will be transfered to the Auction Market and the auction started then  users start placing  BId for this Liquidated Asset and Highest bidders get the Liquidated Asset then we pay back the Lenders funds with his Interest.

 ### 3.  How Does AI Agent work?**

 The AI agent is the agent that assist users in Trading their crypto assets on our AMM and also Assist the Borrowers Request Loans aswell as the Lenders.
